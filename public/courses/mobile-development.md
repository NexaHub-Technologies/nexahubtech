## The idea this course installs: Your app is a guest, not the host

A phone interrupts you. A call arrives, the user switches away, the system reclaims your memory, the network vanishes halfway through a tap. A mobile app isn't a program that runs from start to finish — it is something that has to survive being paused, resumed and quietly destroyed, and still look to the person holding it as though it never left.

Once it lands, you can:

- Treat state as something that must survive being killed
- Design for a slow or absent network as the normal case
- Work with the platform lifecycle instead of against it
