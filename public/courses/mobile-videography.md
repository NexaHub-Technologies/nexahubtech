## The idea this course installs: The edit starts when you press record

On a phone, the camera is never the limit — the decisions are. Where you stand, what is in the frame, where the light comes from and how close the microphone is are all settled before you press record, and no editing app can fix them afterwards. Shoot as if you are already in the edit: hold each shot long enough to cut, get the sound clean, and film the moment before and after. Then editing stops being rescue work and becomes what it should be — choosing.

Once it lands, you can:

- Shoot clips that cut together, instead of clips you have to rescue
- Get clean sound from a phone without buying anything
- Edit a short piece for a real purpose and export it properly
