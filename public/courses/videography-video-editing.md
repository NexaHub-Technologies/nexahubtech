## The idea this course installs: You are directing attention, not recording events

A camera doesn't capture a scene — it excludes everything outside the frame. A light doesn't only brighten; it tells the eye where to go. A cut moves attention from one thing to the next. Once framing, lighting and editing read as three ways of answering the same question — what should the viewer be looking at, and when — technique stops being a list of settings and becomes a set of decisions.

Once it lands, you can:

- Frame a shot by what you leave out, not only what you include
- Cut on the moment attention is ready to move
- Light and grade to lead the eye, not just to look expensive
