## The idea this course installs: Data answers the question you actually asked

Every number in a dataset is the residue of a decision somebody made — what to record, what to leave out, how to count it. Analysis is less about tools than about keeping the question, the data and the claim honestly aligned. The real skill is noticing the gap between what you want to know and what this data can actually tell you.

Once it lands, you can:

- Spot a misleading chart — including one of your own
- Tell a real result from noise, and say which it is
- State plainly what a finding does and does not support
